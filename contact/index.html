Useful context

  • The problemWhat is difficult, slow, risky, or unclear today?
  • The peopleWho uses the system or depends on the outcome?
  • The current stateIs this a new idea, an existing product, or a workflow held together by several tools?

  • ConstraintsInclude timing, existing technology, compliance needs, or other known boundaries if they matter.
