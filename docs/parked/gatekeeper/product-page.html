Gatekeeper

Nothing happens until someone says yes.

Gatekeeper holds a risky action—a payment, a patient file, a contract, or an AI agent’s next step—until the right person approves.

Open Gatekeeper How it works

The operating model

Ask. Decide. Keep.

01

Ask

A person, service, or agent requests permission before the action takes place.

02

Decide

The request reaches the responsible approver with the context needed to make a clear decision.

03

Keep

The decision and its timeline remain available as proof of what happened and why.

Product views

Risky moments, held safely.

Gatekeeper adapts the same approval model to legal, finance, healthcare, DevOps, and other high-consequence work.

What it protects

Approval where the cost of acting first is too high.

Human-in-the-loop actions

Put a responsible person between an automated request and the action it wants to take.

Shared accountability

Give approvers the request, context, and ownership needed to decide without guesswork.

Durable evidence

Keep an understandable timeline of requests, decisions, and related records.

One pattern across teams

Use the same decision model for contracts, payments, operations, and software changes.
