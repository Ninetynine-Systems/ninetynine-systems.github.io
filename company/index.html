Company

Focused by design. Serious about the work.

ninetynine.systems LLC is an independent software company that builds its own products and partners with teams on focused software systems.

What we believe

Useful systems should be understandable, dependable, and built with care.

Focus before breadth

A product earns its place by doing an important job clearly. We start with that job and resist features that blur it.

Reliability is a product feature

Architecture, data, operations, and support are part of the experience—not invisible work left for later.

Decisions should be explainable

We value systems and working relationships where ownership, tradeoffs, and outcomes remain understandable.

Stay close to the user

Good software grows from the actual work, the people doing it, and the constraints they live with.
