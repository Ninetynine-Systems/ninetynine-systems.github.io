01 / Operating standard

Reliability is part of the invention.

New capability matters when it works under real operating conditions. We define authority, failure behavior, and evidence alongside the capability itself.

  1. 01

    Explicit authority

    Ownership and decision rights stay unambiguous across people, agents, and machines.

  2. 02

    Defined failure behavior

    Failures, recovery paths, and refusal modes are defined as primary system behavior.

  3. 03

    Verifiable evidence

    Important actions leave records that can be inspected, understood, and verified.

02 / Flagship system

Gatekeeper.

Nothing happens until someone says yes.

Live

Gatekeeper is approval infrastructure for consequential actions. It pauses a deploy, payment, database change, or agent operation until the right person approves it—then keeps a signed, verifiable record of the decision.
